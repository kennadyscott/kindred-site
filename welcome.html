Founding Therapist

You’re in.

Your membership is active and your founding rate is locked for your first 12 months. Here’s exactly what happens next — it takes about ten minutes of your time, and then it’s on us.

  1. 1

    Build your profile about 10 minutes

    Prompt by prompt — your therapy style, who you work best with, what sessions feel like. You’re never starting from a blank page, and you can save and come back.

    Build my profile →
  2. 2

    Sign in once, then verify your identity and licence

    Use the same email and password you just created. You’ll enter your licence number and the state that issued it, and confirm your identity — every therapist on Kindred goes through this, and it’s what lets a client trust the person they’re reading about.

  3. 3

    We check it

    A real person on our side confirms your licence against your state board. We’ll email you the moment it clears — and if anything doesn’t match, we’ll write to you directly rather than leaving you wondering.

  4. 4

    You go live

    Your profile enters client matching, and you start receiving inquiries from people whose needs actually line up with how you work.

What to have
handy.

◇

Your licence details

Licence number, the issuing state, and your credential (LMFT, LCSW, LPC, PhD/PsyD, or equivalent).

❝

A few sentences in your voice

How you’d describe your approach to someone at a party — not how you’d write it for an insurance panel.

◎

A photo you actually like

Warm and human beats formal. It’s the first thing a nervous person looks at.

✳

Your practical details

Location, in-person or online, insurance or private pay, and whether you’re taking new clients.

You joined before this was proven.

That’s not lost on us. Founding therapists shape what Kindred becomes — the profile prompts, the fit tags, how matching works. If something feels wrong or missing, reply to any email we send you and it lands with a person, not a queue.

Questions before you start?

Two things people usually ask.

Can I finish this later?

Yes. Your progress saves as you go, and your membership is already active — nothing expires while you’re drafting.

What if my licence check has a problem?

We email you directly and help sort it. Nothing is silently rejected, and you can cancel for a full refund if we can’t verify you.

Something else?

Write to us and a person answers.
